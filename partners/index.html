01 / Ways to collaborate

What we're looking for

Talks, Workshops & Mentorship

Spend an evening with the club. Deliver a technical deep-dive, bring a raw dataset for us to attack together, or provide an honest look at the day-to-day reality of the industry. We also welcome ongoing mentorship to guide our members' research.

02 / What we can offer

Direct access to quantitative talent.

Kwant Klubben is a community of students at SDU who are self-selected for quantitative finance. People from economics, mathematics, computer science, and engineering who choose to spend their free time on rigorous research and coding.

What we offer is early and direct contact with these individuals, along with a transparent public library of their research and strategies you can review.

If your goal is to discover and connect with the next generation of quantitative talent, we are the place to look.

03 / Partnership tiers

Three ways to back the club.

Principal partner

One per year. The club's main backer.

  • First and largest logo on the site and in every event deck
  • Your name on the workshop series ("… workshop, with X")
  • Two events a semester, co-designed with the board
  • A standing channel for your openings on the club Discord
  • Sector exclusivity: no competing principal partner
Partner

Companies who want regular contact with members.

  • Logo on the site and in event decks
  • One event a semester: a talk, case or workshop
  • Openings shared on the club Discord
Supporter

Backing a single event, or giving in kind: data, software, a venue.

  • Logo on the site
  • Thanked at the events you support
